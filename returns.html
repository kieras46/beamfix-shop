Returns, Refunds & Warranty

Last updated:

In short: you can return unused items within days of delivery for a refund. Faulty items are refunded or replaced at our cost. Adjuster kits marked Lifetime Warranty are replaced free if they ever break under normal use.

1. Changed your mind?

By law you have 14 days from the day after delivery to cancel an online order without giving a reason. We give you longer: days from the day after delivery.

Your refund

  • We refund the price of the goods and the standard delivery charge you paid. If you chose a more expensive delivery option, we only refund the cost of our cheapest standard delivery.
  • For change-of-mind returns, you pay the cost of sending the goods back.
  • We refund within 14 days of receiving the goods back or receiving proof that you have sent them, whichever is earlier. Refunds go back to your original payment method.
  • You may handle the goods as you would in a shop to check them. If they have been fitted, damaged or used beyond that, we may reduce the refund to reflect the loss in value.

2. Faulty, damaged or wrong item

If an item arrives faulty or damaged, or is not what you ordered, email us with your order number and a photo. We will pay for the return (or may not need the item back) and will, at your choice and as the law allows:

  • within 30 days of delivery, refund you in full; or
  • send a replacement, or refund you if a replacement is not possible.

These rights come from the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and are not affected by this policy or our warranty.

3. Lifetime Warranty

Our headlight adjuster kits are moulded from PA6 nylon and carry a Lifetime Warranty where stated in the product title.

CoveredA part we supplied that cracks, snaps or fails under normal use, for as long as you own the vehicle it was fitted to.
What you getA free replacement part, sent free of charge to a UK address. International customers may be asked to pay postage.
Not coveredDamage from incorrect fitting or over-tightening, accidents or impact, modification, use on a vehicle or headlight type the part is not listed for (e.g. xenon units where excluded), labour or fitting costs, and the cost of other parts of the headlamp.
How to claimEmail with your order number and a photo of the broken part. You do not normally need to return it.

4. Exceptions

We cannot accept change-of-mind returns of items that have been made to your specification or clearly personalised. Business customers do not have the change-of-mind cancellation right.
