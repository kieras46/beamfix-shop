Why our adjusters don't break again

The original parts were made from brittle plastic that cracks with heat and age. Ours are moulded from PA6 nylon.

01

Diagnose

If your beam points at the road, wobbles over bumps or won't adjust, the adjuster ball-joint or cog has snapped.

Pick your car. Most kits come in pairs and repair two headlights. Cogs and gears are also sold separately.

FAQ

Do these fit xenon headlights?

Jaguar S-Type kits are for halogen headlights only, not xenon. Check the product description or contact us if you are unsure.

How many headlights does one kit repair?

It depends on the listing: "1 set" repairs one headlight, "2 sets" repair two. A full pair of headlights may need 4 cogs, so check the cog count in the title.

What does the lifetime warranty cover?

If a part we supplied breaks under normal use, we send a replacement free of charge. Just contact us with your order number.

How fast is delivery?

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. UK delivery usually takes 1–3 working days, international 5–10.

Can I return an item?

Yes. Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. See our Returns & Warranty policy.
